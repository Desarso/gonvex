Why 182ms is the evidenced floor

Fixed delays removed

Idle invalidations begin immediately after authoritative committed triggers.

Queue stays empty

Rerun wait and peak depth remained zero throughout certification.

GC + runner cleared

STW <2ms in the controlled gate; generator peak 0.807 cores.

Work scales with rows

Tail rises as tasks grow; DB/result construction and fanout dominate.

Safety boundary: no optimization is accepted if it can miss a visible row change, reuse another identity's result, weaken digest/revision checks, or propagate uncommitted state. The remaining gap is not permission to cross that boundary.

Work retained

Trigger path

Immediate committed invalidation

The first PostgreSQL trigger observation gets a 1ms micro-batch; only in-flight storms coalesce. Commit and trigger ordering remain authoritative.

Execution

Exact visibility sharing

Identical executions share only across complete visibility fingerprints. Resolver doubt and audience mismatch fail closed to identity-specific work.

Protocol

Capability-gated patches

Keyed rows, order deltas, object collections, and per-connection fanout reduce bytes. Legacy, stale, or unsupported clients receive full results.

Application

Indexed task-log queries

Task logs use indexed taskId reads and the exact task-visibility resolver; server average fell from roughly 80ms to 12–16ms.

No-op

No delivery, no revision drift

A semantically unchanged invalidation emits no progress and advances no revision, preserving the client's exact next patch base. The final suite also restored the empty slaPolicies fallback key.

Fanout

Measured physical delivery

Immediate, uncompressed, 32-worker delivery with 5k groups won. 64 workers, compression, 10ms batching, and a commit barrier regressed or coupled paths and were removed.
